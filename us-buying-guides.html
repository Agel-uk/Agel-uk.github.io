A three-step buying method

  1. Start with your actual use case. List the rooms, devices or routines the product must support.
  2. Choose two or three must-have features. Avoid paying extra for specifications that will not change your experience.
  3. Verify the current listing. Check the seller, compatibility, warranty, returns, availability and final price at the retailer before ordering.
