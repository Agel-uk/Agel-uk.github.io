Does DealLens AI sell products?

No. Purchases take place on third-party retailer websites.

Are prices live?

We do not present static guide content as live pricing. Verify current price, availability, seller, shipping and return terms on the retailer page.

Does DealLens AI use affiliate links?

Some links may use approved affiliate tracking. DealLens AI may earn a commission from qualifying purchases without increasing your price.

How are guides created?

Guides emphasize practical criteria, compatibility, ownership costs, trade-offs and common buying mistakes. See our Editorial Policy.
