Make better buying decisions before comparing products

Understand the trade-offs first, then compare current products and retailer terms.

Define the job

Write down what the product must do, where it will be used, what it must work with and which limitations would make it a bad purchase.

Separate durable criteria from changing data

Compatibility, ergonomics, capacity and ownership costs are durable criteria. Price, stock, seller identity and delivery dates change frequently and should be checked at the retailer.

Compare total ownership

Accessories, subscriptions, replacement parts, consumables, warranty terms and upgradeability can change the real cost.

Use the right guide

Browse the buying guide library for category-specific checklists and common mistakes.

Verify before purchasing

Confirm the exact model, seller, warranty, return policy, current price and availability on the retailer page.
