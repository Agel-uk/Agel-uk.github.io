EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

DealLens AI creates decision-first buying guides intended to help people understand trade-offs before visiting a retailer.

Editorial approach

We focus on practical criteria, compatibility, ownership considerations and common buying mistakes. We do not invent live prices, ratings, stock levels or commission figures.

Affiliate relationships

Some outbound links may use approved affiliate tracking. A qualifying purchase may generate a commission without increasing the shopper's price. Affiliate eligibility does not determine the criteria explained in our guides.

Updates

Guides may be revised when standards, technologies or common buyer considerations materially change. Current price, availability, seller identity and delivery terms should always be verified at the retailer.

AI-assisted workflow

DealLens AI may use AI-assisted tools to help organize, draft or improve editorial material. The goal is useful category-level guidance rather than mass-produced search-engine filler.
